Jade Lantern Market sources tea, silk, porcelain and everyday electronics directly from small workshops and factories across China. We handle the sourcing, quality checks, and shipping, so you get the real thing without the guesswork.

Started with one suitcase of tea samples.

Jade Lantern Market began when our founder brought back a suitcase of oolong tea from a trip to Fujian and started sharing it with friends and neighbors. The response was immediate: people wanted to know where to buy more.

Today we work with a small network of family-run workshops and larger factories that we've visited and vetted in person. Every product on our site has been sampled by our team before it's listed.
